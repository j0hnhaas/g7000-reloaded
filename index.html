G7000 Reloaded.

Preserving the building blocks of early video games — and making them accessible for exploration, research, and new creative work.

An independent research project dedicated to the Philips Videopac G7000 / Magnavox Odyssey². We aim to identify and document the visual and audio elements of its games, celebrate the ingenuity of early game design, and encourage new encounters with video-game heritage.

Research directions

01 / InventoryDocument ROMs, versions, provenance and checksums.
02 / VisualsInvestigate characters, sprites, animations and graphic patterns.
03 / SoundsStudy sound registers, effects and reproducible sequences.
04 / New creationsDevelop accessible tools for exploring and building new games.
